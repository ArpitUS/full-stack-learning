# Module 66: Senior and Architect Interview

Prepare to discuss real engineering work at the level of architecture, collaboration, trade-offs, reliability, mentoring, and technical leadership. Base stories on TeamOps and actual learning evidence.

## Practice Deliverable

Create a story bank with at least eight concise records across architecture, code review, incident response, debugging, technical debt, prioritization, mentoring/collaboration, and disagreement/trade-off. Conduct one mock architecture review of TeamOps using its architecture, stages, backlog, and contribution evidence.

## Story Template

```md
# Story: Topic

- Situation and context:
- Constraint/problem:
- Options considered:
- Action and technical rationale:
- Result and evidence:
- What I would improve:
- Follow-up question I can answer:
```

## Completion Criteria

- [ ] Explain the TeamOps architecture and why it starts as a modular monolith.
- [ ] Defend a decision with requirements, trade-offs, and evidence.
- [ ] Describe an incident or failure honestly, including prevention work.
- [ ] Give constructive code-review feedback with a technical rationale.
- [ ] Explain how to reduce technical debt without stopping all product work.

## Common Mistakes

Invented stories, tool-first explanations, claiming sole ownership of team work, no measurable result, no reflection on mistakes, and describing leadership only as assigning work.
